Privacy
Policy.

Effective October 18, 2026

Who we are

Reedy Moving Co. is an owner-operated moving labor company in Travelers Rest, South Carolina. This policy covers reedymoving.com and how we handle information you give us by form, phone, text or email.

What we collect

Only what you send us:

  • Quote requests: your name, phone number, email (optional), the addresses you're moving from and to, your move date, home size, the services you need and any notes.
  • Partner referrals: the referring partner's name, firm, phone and email, plus the client's name, phone and move details the partner chooses to share, and a mailing address if you ask us to send cards.
  • Calls, texts and emails: your number or address and whatever you tell us about the job.

We don't ask for payment details on this site. Payments are handled separately through our payment processor.

How we use it

  • To send you a rate and not-to-exceed cap, schedule your job, and follow up about it.
  • To text or call you about your move. Texts are about your job only: quotes, scheduling, arrival times and a follow-up afterward.
  • For partner referrals, to contact the client you referred and to let you know when the job is done.

Text messages

If you give us your number, you agree we can text you about your quote and your move. Message frequency depends on your job, usually a handful of messages. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We never send marketing texts without asking first.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or text opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the services we use to run the business, and only as needed to do the job:

  • our website host and form-delivery service, which pass your form submission to us;
  • our phone and texting provider;
  • our payment processor, when you pay for a job;
  • anyone the law requires us to share it with.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics that count visits and pages viewed without identifying you.

How long we keep it

We keep job records for as long as we need them to serve you and for our tax and business records. If you never book, we delete your quote request when it's no longer useful.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call or text 864.280.7243 or email David@reedymoving.com.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
